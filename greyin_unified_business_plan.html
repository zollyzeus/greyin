1. The Three Pillars Architecture

Pillar 1: GREYIN (B2B Enterprise Portal)

Outward-facing corporate marketplace for on-demand senior experts, principal architects, and fractional leaders. Enables hiring managers to search age-blind talent cards and contract experienced leaders without paying full-time executive recruiter overhead.

Pillar 2: SALT & PEPPER (Community Lounge)

An embedded, gated social space for verified members. Features private discussion boards, code/architecture design reviews, project showcases ("The Lab"), and informal peer-to-peer referral circles without corporate recruiter noise.

Pillar 3: FREEAGENT (0%-Commission Freelancing Engine)

A zero-fee freelance ecosystem supporting community builders. Members can showcase MVPs, offer micro-consulting services, or contract peer-to-peer while retaining 100% of their contract earnings.

3. Self-Hosted Tech Stack & NocoBase Blueprint

The entire ecosystem is designed to run 100% free and self-hosted via Docker using open-source tools:

  • NocoBase 2.0 (App Builder): Manages visual relational collections, multi-space layouts, and RBAC permissions.
  • PostgreSQL 16 (Database): Secure relational storage for verified member profiles, project showcases, and B2B contracts.
  • Authentik / Keycloak (Identity): Local OIDC/OAuth provider handling age-blind experience verification workflows.
  • Meilisearch (Search Engine): Fast, age-blind capability matching across member skills and domain depth.

4. Strategic Growth & Flywheel Effect

  1. Talent Acquisition: Senior talent joins for free peer interaction in Salt & Pepper and 0%-fee freelancing in FreeAgent.
  2. Proof of Work: Members showcase real open-source systems, architectures, and tools in the Lounge.
  3. Enterprise Attraction: Corporate clients pay Greyin to access a verified pool of active practitioners.
  4. Reinvestment: B2B revenue funds platform infrastructure and community support.
