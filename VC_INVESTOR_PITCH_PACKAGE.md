## ❓ COMMON INVESTOR QUESTIONS

**Q: How does this compare to existing competitors (LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Indeed)?**
A: Those platforms are siloed. LinkedIn ≠ salary data, Glassdoor ≠ gig work, Indeed ≠ peer verification. Greyin combines all three with AI-powered scoring—no competitor does this.

**Q: What's your moat?**
A: Bayesian shrinkage scoring algorithm (defensible, patent-pending). Network effects from peer verification. Cost advantage (10-50x infrastructure savings).

**Q: How do you acquire users?**
A: 40% referral (professionals invite peers), 25% content (salary reports), 20% direct sales (HR/recruiting), 15% partnerships (job boards, networks).

**Q: What's your path to profitability?**
A: Break-even at 8K DAU (Month 15-18). Positive contribution margin at 1K DAU (Month 6). Full profitability by Month 18. No perpetual burn.

**Q: How much capital do you need?**
A: $800K-1.2M seed round for 12-16 month runway to break-even. Then self-sustaining or Series A from profitability.

**Q: What are your biggest risks?**
A: Data privacy (mitigated via k-anonymity), low adoption (mitigated via referrals), competitive response (defensible moat), regulatory (compliance-first approach).
