1. Recommended Tech Stack (100% Free & Open-Source)

To meet your requirement of a free, open-source, and self-hosted stack capable of complex relational data (users, fractional projects, peer endorsements, feeds, and referrals), we recommend an integrated low-code microservice architecture:

Primary Framework: NocoBase (or Baserow) + PostgreSQL

  • Core Platform — NocoBase (Self-Hosted via Docker): Unlike basic form-builders, NocoBase is a extensible open-source no-code/low-code web application platform. It natively supports granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), multi-table relations, visual page layout creation, and flexible workflow automation.
  • Database — PostgreSQL: Enterprise-grade open-source relational database storing encrypted user profiles, project listings, and peer referral graphs.
  • Self-Hosted Auth & Identity — Authentik / Keycloak: Provides secure passwordless login, OIDC/OAuth2, and strict verification workflows without vendor lock-in.
  • Deployment Target: Local Docker Compose / On-Premises Server or low-cost Linux VPS ($5–$10/mo).

2. Business Plan Strategy

2.1 Market Positioning & Legal Compliance

To avoid app-store policy violations and public accommodation civil rights issues regarding age-gating, the platform uses an Experience-Based Threshold instead of biological age:

  • Qualification Rule: Gate entry to individuals with 12+ years of verified professional experience or senior/executive titles (Lead, Staff, Manager, Director, Architect, VP).
  • Positioning: "The Executive Advisory & Peer Referral Network for Senior Domain Experts."

2.2 Monetization Architecture

Fractional Marketplace Take-Fee / Commission 10% fee on fractional C-suite/Architect consulting contracts.
Enterprise Outplacement B2B SaaS License Corporate licenses purchased by companies undergoing downsizing.

3. Product Requirements Document (PRD)

3.1 Functional Requirements Matrix

Module ID Feature Name Description & Business Logic Priority
FR-01 Age-Blind Talent Card Profiles showcase verified domain achievements, skills, and project history. Graduation years, birthdates, and photo requirements are completely omitted to neutralize ATS bias. High
FR-02 Peer Referral Bridge Gated introduction engine allowing senior members to directly refer peers into active openings at their current/former firms, bypassing cold ATS applications. High
FR-03 Fractional & Advisory Engine Marketplace listing short-term interim roles, advisory board posts, and part-time domain consultant requests from startups and SMBs. Medium
FR-04 Domain-to-AI Upskilling Hub Curated repository and discussion threads focused on leveraging modern generative AI tools to amplify 15+ years of domain expertise. Medium

3.2 Technical & Security Architecture Requirements

  • Data Isolation & Self-Hosting: All data stored in locally hosted Docker volumes with automated encrypted pg_dump backups.
  • Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):
    • Unverified Applicant: Access restricted to onboarding form and identity upload.
    • Verified Veteran Member: Full access to search talent, post articles, send referral requests, and bid on fractional work.
    • Corporate Client / Recruiter: Access restricted to viewing anonymous capability cards and posting fractional roles.
  • Compliance: Fully compliant with GDPR and local data protection standards by enabling complete profile deletion and zero tracking telemetry.
