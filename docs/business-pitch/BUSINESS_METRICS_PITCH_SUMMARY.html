Revenue Assumptions

Cost Assumptions

  • AWS Infrastructure: Scales non-linearly; compute, database, storage, networking, monitoring — full 7-milestone model in docs/business-pitch/Greyin_Budget_Rampup_3Year.xlsx
  • Engineering Team: India-based (Bangalore), product-company tier; 10 roles ranging ₹50K-458K/month (real 2026 market rates, not IT-services tier)

  • Operating Costs: SaaS tools ($300/mo), DevOps ($200/mo), communication ($150/mo), etc.
  • Contingency: Budget does not include 15-20% buffer; recommend adding for safety

Key Metrics Definitions

  • ARPU (Annual Revenue Per User): Total annual revenue ÷ total users
  • CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost): Total acquisition spend ÷ new customers acquired
  • LTV (Lifetime Value): Average revenue per user × retention period (e.g., 2 years)
  • Churn Rate: % of customers who cease subscription in a given period
  • NRR (Net Revenue Retention): (Beginning MRR + Expansion - Churn) ÷ Beginning MRR × 100%
  • Contribution Margin: Total revenue - variable costs (AWS, LLM, payment processing)
